The product

Professor Idris teaches inside each course in two modes: Professor (lesson by lesson, with a verification after every section and remediation when a check fails) and Troubleshooting (unblocking, anchored to the catalogue's own courses — the learner's misconception is diagnosed, a guiding question comes before any full correction).

A living catalogue. 788 certification lines across all domains; 589 modules already closed before an external jury, each a complete kit — course, exercises, corrigés, labs, and an original mock exam the system itself sat and passed under jury control. The catalogue grows daily; it is presented as living, not as finished.

The model: per-course purchase. The candidate buys the course for THEIR certification — course + exercises + labs + mock exam, taught by Professor Idris. Each course carries its own price; the founder's governing example is US$50 per course, against traditional training that can cost $3,000 or more. There is no recurring plan. All resources — videos, PDFs, exercises — stay inside the platform, view-only.
